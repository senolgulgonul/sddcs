Specification

Training

Certify runs a zero-tolerance shrinking-grid search (a coarse full-range scan, then up to eight separated candidates each refined by six shrinking stages; gains in log scale, poles and zeros dense near z = 1). Global grid: order 1 over gain, zero, pole. Cancellation ansatz: order 2 with one zero placed on the discrete plant pole (or both zeros on a complex pair) and the rest searched, the distilled parametrization of the paper. Around trained solution: local search seeded by the best trained controller (any order 1 or 2 result). With the integrator box checked the integrator pole is pinned at z₁ = 1 (required for case C).
Loss: smoothed settling + λ·TV₀ continuation (300 → 3000 → 30000) + tail term + bound term; overshoot hinge when monotonicity is off. Gradients are exact (forward sensitivities), clipped at 1. Integrator start places only the spec-mandated pole; gains and z₂ stay random (uncheck for the fully random ablation).
